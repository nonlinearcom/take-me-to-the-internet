This issue of Networked Magazine is the outcome of a workshop held in
the PhD program in Visual and Media Studies at IULM University Milan.
Participants crafted together an online publication, curating articles
that showcased their ongoing research practices. The purpose of the
hands-on workshop was not only educational but also initiatory. It
offered a hands-on introduction to HTML, CSS and Javascript
fundamentals, enabling participants to apply coding strategies to their
articles.

Networked Magazine is a project that thrives on novel ways for making
the web and hands-on approaches to a web in the making. It offers a
workshop-oriented approach to online publishing by enabling connections
between coding fundamentals and the newest perspectives in visual design
and digital culture.
